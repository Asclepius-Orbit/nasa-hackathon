## Reproducibility and interpretation

To refresh this inventory, inspect each linked source and its rights notice,
record the access date, publication version, exact page, population, posture,
units, collection method and limitations, and retain provenance for each
excerpt that is actually distributed. Re-check third-party illustrations and
licences separately; online access alone does not grant redistribution rights.
Preserve missing or unverified fields as such. An individual 1g baseline must
match the measured posture and quantity. Stature, seated height and an estimated
sensor cord length are distinct; none is interchangeable without a validated
conversion. Exercise, sensor uncertainty and assumptions need explicit treatment.
